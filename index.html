Length of pause
How long a silence has to last before the caller answers. Longer is safer — you can pause between sentences without being cut off, and press Send (or Enter) the moment you have finished.

Microphone sensitivity

Calibrate to your room
Takes eight seconds. Stay quiet for the first half, then talk normally the way you would on a call.
If people talking nearby keep getting picked up, move this toward Strict — only speech close to the microphone counts. Move toward Sensitive if your own quiet speech is being ignored.

Speech recognition

If the app mishears you often, try another English variant — recognition accuracy changes a lot between them. Takes effect on the next call.
